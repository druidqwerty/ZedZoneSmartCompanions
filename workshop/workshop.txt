description=[h2]New in 3.2.3 (Battery Charger & Charcoal Fuel Fix)[/h2]
description=[list][*]Fixed Battery Charger rotation loop: companions now read actual battery charge (RemainingBattery Wh) instead of casing durability and respect native charger cell states — partially charged batteries stay charging until 100%, fully charged Vehicle Batteries are no longer reloaded into chargers, and chargers stay excluded from regular chest sorting.[*]Fixed Charcoal stuck in furnaces and generators: companions now verify native Combustible fuel flags before loading Stirling Generators, Heaters, and Stone Furnaces (never feeding non-combustible Charcoal or wooden weapons), use exact station input/output/fuel slots, and clear accumulated Charcoal/ash from fuel slots when space runs low.[*]Includes all 3.2.2–3.2.0 updates: opt-in Event Log Recording & [!] Mark Bug in F8 -> Settings, Fever/Cold companion freeze fix, automatic NPC gear repair across all 18 equipped slots and nested bags, and full Patch 1.0.20 support.[/list]
description=[h2]Quick Start: How to Use[/h2]
description=[list][*]1. Press F8 (or click [?] inside F8) to open the squad and base hub. In Squad, pick doctrines, formations, [A]/[B] fireteams, or toggle Garrison to leave guards at camp.[*]2. Open any base chest (or use F8 -> Base) to tick storage categories on destination chests and mark one chest as [Drop-off] — companions only sort between player-marked chests.[*]3. Dump loot into [Drop-off] and press F7: companions physically walk and carry items into matching chests (no teleporting). In Work, pick presets (Fighter/Medic/Supplier/Scavenger) and toggles like Auto-harvest crops; in Settings, choose NPC Gear Auto-Repair mode.[*]Hotkeys: F8 hub · Alt+1/2/3 All/Alpha/Bravo · Alt+F sweep nearby loot · F7 physical sort · F6 (Alt+F6 with AVO) share rations · Alt+G move to cursor · Alt+H healing smoke · F9 battle cry.[/list]
