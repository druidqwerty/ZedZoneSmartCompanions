description=[h2]Quick Start: How to Use[/h2]
description=[list][*]1. Press F8 (or click [?] inside F8) to open the squad and base hub. In Squad, pick doctrines, formations, [A]/[B] fireteams, or toggle Garrison to leave guards at camp.[*]2. Open any base chest (or use F8 -> Base) to tick storage categories on destination chests and mark one chest as [Drop-off] — companions only sort between player-marked chests.[*]3. Dump loot into [Drop-off] and press F7: companions physically walk and carry items into matching chests (no teleporting). In Work, pick presets (Fighter/Medic/Supplier/Scavenger) and toggles like Auto-harvest crops; in Settings, choose NPC Gear Auto-Repair mode.[*]Hotkeys: F8 hub · Alt+1/2/3 All/Alpha/Bravo · Alt+F sweep nearby loot · F7 physical sort · F6 (Alt+F6 with AVO) share rations · Alt+G move to cursor · Alt+H healing smoke · F9 battle cry.[/list]
