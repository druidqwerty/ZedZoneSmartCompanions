description=[h2]Core features (1.1.0 & 1.3.0)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Post-Apocalyptic Profanity & Attitude Mode (18+ Rude / Mixed / Clean):[/b] switch between [i]Hardcore 18+[/i] (default), [i]Mixed[/i], and [i]Clean (Streamer)[/i] anytime in [b]F8[/b] across Russian (495 lines), English (229 lines), and Chinese (263 lines).
description=[*][b]Share Ration & "Fed & Fierce" Buff ([b]F6[/b]):[/b] press [b]F6[/b] (or enable [i]Auto-Share Food When Eating[/i] in [b]F8[/b]) to share a meal with your squad—restoring hunger/thirst/stamina, boosting loyalty, and granting the 3-minute [b]"Fed & Fierce"[/b] combat buff.
description=[*][b]Smarter combat vs. zombies & hostile NPCs:[/b] companions prioritize threats attacking you at close range, recognize armed human raiders and giant mutants, kite backward while firing, strafe against enemy shooters, warn you about rear flankers, clear jammed guns, step out of your line of fire ([b]Anti-Friendly-Fire corridor[/b]), and swap between melee and firearms dynamically.
description=[*][b]Base chest sorting, Auto-Dropoff & 12-category UI:[/b] opening any chest, locker, or fridge displays a 12-category bar at the top of the screen plus a [b]Drop-off Box[/b] toggle and 1-click presets. Press [b]F7[/b] to order companions to sort your base storage, or walk near base chests out of combat for automatic raid loot drop-off.
description=[*][b]6 Tactical Doctrines & Field Support ([b]F8[/b]):[/b] switch between [i]Balanced[/i], [i]Assault[/i], [i]Bodyguard[/i], [i]Sniper/Kite[/i], [i]Stealth Melee[/i], and [i]Passive[/i], plus [b]Field Medic[/b] and [b]Field Ammo Supplier[/b].
description=[/list]
